About Us

Driven by Precision.
Built on Trust.

Located in Chimbali, Chakan (Pune), Strength Polymer Industries is a trusted manufacturer of high-quality rubber components for diverse industrial sectors. Founded by Maruti Pawar with 20+ years of expertise, every product reflects our values of precision, durability, and reliability.

Our ISO 9001:2015 certified facility is equipped with advanced compressor rubber molding machines ensuring strict dimensional tolerances and consistent quality — from raw material selection to final inspection.
